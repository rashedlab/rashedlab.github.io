Biography

About

Prof. Essam Rashed leads the Rashed Lab and develops AI systems for medical imaging and clinical decision support. His work spans segmentation, diagnosis, uncertainty estimation, report generation, multimodal reasoning, and privacy-preserving collaborative intelligence. He is particularly interested in designing clinically meaningful AI pipelines that are not only accurate, but also transparent, robust, and deployable in real-world healthcare settings.

His research combines imaging data, clinical text, domain knowledge, and modern foundation models to support problems ranging from lesion analysis and radiological reporting to agentic workflows and regulatory-aware evaluation. The overarching goal is to bridge advanced machine learning with practical medical impact.
